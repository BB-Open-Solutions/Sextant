**The situation you are in**
What you are trying to do with your fleet, and where Sextant makes it hard.
Start here rather than with a solution - the problem is the part we cannot
guess, and it often has an answer we did not expect.

**What you do today instead**
A workaround, a script, another tool, or nothing.

**How you would know it was solved**
What would be true afterwards that is not true now.

**Scale**
How many devices, how many device classes, how many people administering them.
Sextant's costs scale with configuration SHAPES rather than device count
(docs/architecture/scale.md), so this changes the answer more than it looks.
